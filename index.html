The Merit
Board Loop

Monthly exams make students study for two weeks a year. We replace them with 15-25 high-intensity quizzes every hour, marked live by the students themselves.

Full breakdown
01

Multi-TV Deployment

A class with multiple TVs is created to handle different learning levels. Students are divided into groups and seated in front of the screen matching their current level.

02

Rapid Fire Quizzing

A set of rapid quizzes is displayed on TV, and students check the competing students' answers on the spot.

03

The Merit Slide

Winners, if any, are moved up on the merit board and seats are exchanged. Between 15 and 25 quizzes are administered in an hour's time.

04

Volume Practice

Even if students do not study by themselves, the practice provided by 100-150 questions makes them familiar with the subject.

Our Mission

Access to best quality education for all, without the excuse of heavy investment required for quality education.

We supplement lecture-based teaching with a low-cost, self-driven practice system. In the traditional model students are tested monthly, so they pay attention only when exams are close. The Kant Process nudges them to pay attention all the time.
